# Pruthvi Raj Eranti

Engineering leader and builder in Bengaluru. For the last five years I was co-founder and CTO of Cogrow Ventures (Twig & iTribe), where we built trading platforms for Indian brokers. Before that I was a Staff Engineer at Walmart Global Tech, and I've worked at Google, Disney and BlackBuck. CS from IIT Kharagpur.

## Things I've built

- **A multi-broker trading terminal.** We put 15 Indian broker APIs behind one interface, and the platform ran white-labelled for about 75K traders a day at 99.9% uptime. Four engineers carried web, mobile and every partner deployment, mostly because we rebuilt how the team works around AI coding agents.
- **iTribe**, our consumer app, which reached 100K monthly users and 10M+ requests a month at under 100ms P99. I also cut our cloud spend by about 90%.
- **Walmart's customer identity graph**, 115B nodes, resolving one shopper across every touchpoint. US Patent [11,556,370](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11556370B2).

## What I'm working on now

- **[Disclosed](https://github.com/pruthvi-itribe/disclosed)**: real-time NSE/BSE corporate filings, where an LLM proposes claims and every claim is string-matched against the filing before it is shown. It's still early and there's a lot left to do.
- Options research on NIFTY and SPY, mostly backtesting short-strangle rules against real bid/ask data (private for now).
- MCP servers and agent tooling for market data.

If you're working on Indian market data, broker integrations, or LLM extraction that has to be verifiable, I'd be happy to compare notes.
